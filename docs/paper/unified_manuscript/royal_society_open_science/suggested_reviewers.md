## Conflict check before submission

Use these names only if the author has no personal relationship, recent institutional overlap, recent collaboration, shared grant, or supervisory relationship with the proposed reviewer. The Royal Society identifies recent supervisor, mentor, mentee, close-collaborator, and joint-grant relationships—typically within the past three years—as potential competing interests. None of these researchers appears to be an author of the primary lychee datasets analysed in the manuscript, but the author should confirm all personal and unpublished relationships before entering the names in the submission system.
