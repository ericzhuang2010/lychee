Dear Eric Zhuang:

I am writing you in regard to manuscript PHYTOFR-09-26-0099-R entitled "Cultivar-dependent transcriptional responses of lychee to Peronophythora litchii: a registered genome-wide analysis", which you submitted to PhytoFrontiers.

I have decided to not send this manuscript out for review.  Reading the abstract and introduction, it is very hard, impossible actually, for me to understand what you did or what you discovered that is of biological interest.  I am, I concede,  not an expert in this field, but the abstract at least should be understandable to someone  with a basic understanding of plant pathology, genetics and transcriptomics (like me).  There may be a more technical journal focusing on transcriptomics for which this manuscript is suitable.

Thank you for considering PhytoFrontiers for the publication of your research.  I hope the outcome of this specific submission will not discourage you from the submission of future manuscripts.
