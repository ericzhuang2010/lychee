Bioinformatics studies that are based on the analysis of previously published datasets must answer a biologically or medically relevant question not considered in the original publication (if there is one) or reassess the data to arrive at a different conclusion AND comprehensively and robustly validate their interpretation using new experimental data (see https://peerj.com/about/policies-and-procedures/#discipline-standards). In addition, such studies must not duplicate other similar publications and must make a meaningful contribution to the literature.

Unfortunately, your submission does not meet these requirements and so we will not be able to consider it.
