## Author actions required before submission

- Confirm the author name, affiliation, e-mail and ORCID on the title page and in the portal.
- Confirm that the manuscript is not published and is not under consideration elsewhere.
- Confirm the funding, conflict-of-interest, ethics, data-availability and AI-use statements.
- Verify the date and signature already present in the declaration, then export or scan it to PDF.
- Check that each suggested reviewer has no conflict of interest, close personal relationship, supervisory relationship, or recent collaboration with the author. Use at least three reviewers from different institutions and countries.
- Confirm that Zenodo record `22436625` is public and represents the intended submission version.
- If the portal requires a department within the affiliation, supply the preferred department; none was identified in the source materials.
